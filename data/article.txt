The city council has approved a plan to renovate the central library. The work will begin in June and is expected to take six months. The building will receive new computers, improved lighting and a larger children's reading area. During the renovation, residents can borrow books from a temporary library in the community center. The project will cost two million dollars and will be paid for by the city's public services budget. Library staff will also organize weekly reading activities at local schools while the main building is closed.
